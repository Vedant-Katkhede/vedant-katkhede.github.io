About Me

I like infrastructure that is written down, versioned and repeatable.

I'm a B.Tech Computer Science and Engineering student at MIT Art, Design and Technology University, Pune, and cloud is the part of computing I enjoy most. I'm training in AWS and DevOps through a hands-on program at DurgaSoft Solutions, where I build every project end to end in real AWS lab environments.

My work so far covers the full delivery path: provisioning VPCs, EKS clusters and RDS with Terraform, packaging apps with Docker and Helm, shipping them through Jenkins, GitHub Actions and ArgoCD, and watching them with Prometheus, Grafana and CloudWatch. I'm looking for a Cloud / DevOps internship where I can keep doing that on real systems.
